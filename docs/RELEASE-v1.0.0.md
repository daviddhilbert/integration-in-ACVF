First stable release of the Lean formalization of Yimu Yin's
*Integration in algebraically closed valued fields*
([arXiv:0809.0473v2](https://arxiv.org/abs/0809.0473v2)), covering the author's
agreed scope of Sections 2–7.

## Mathematical contents

The construction replaces definable sets in the valued field **VF** by data in
the leading-term sort **RV**. Lifting an RV object recovers a VF set; integration
reverses this passage after identifying the RV presentations that give the same
VF geometry. The central theorems determine those identifications and show that
the resulting integral satisfies Fubini and change of variables.

- **Atomic geometry and preparation (Sections 2–4).** Finite-image and
  volume-descent results, atomic sets and parametric balls, Gamma- and RV-linearity,
  open-to-open partitions, and definable two-cell decomposition supply the
  geometric foundations.
- **Contraction to RV (Section 5).** Special transformations produce standard
  contractions. The comparison of contraction orders makes the resulting RV
  class independent of the choices used in the construction.
- **The kernel without volume forms (Section 6).** Common blowups describe the
  equivalence of lifted RV objects. This gives ordinary integration on
  Grothendieck semirings, integration in definable families, coordinate Fubini,
  and the corresponding localization results.
- **Volume forms and integration (Section 7).** The measured lifting kernel is
  the principal ideal generated by the basic **point-minus-cone relation**.
  Theorem 7.13 identifies the VF volume ring with the resulting RV quotient.
  Propositions 7.11–7.12 prove supported Fubini and change of variables over the
  full coefficient semiring and its Grothendieck ring; Theorems 7.14–7.15 give
  their normalized counterparts.

## Scope and corrections

The measured categories use the adopted **weighted degree-zero convention**:
zero-dimensional objects retain arbitrary nonzero definable RV-valued forms,
isomorphisms preserve those weights exactly, and products multiply both forms.
This supplies the weighted degree-zero coefficients needed in the degree-one
factorization of the principal-kernel argument. Ordinary degree zero is unchanged.

RV[k] has finite-to-one volume maps, with arbitrary auxiliary RV arity.
Definition 5.5 uses explicit RV presentation charts. Lemma 7.5 requires a
nonempty selected-coordinate set. Function integration is stated for positive
VF dimension, with nonempty retained-coordinate projections and a separate
total integral. The model, characteristic, saturation and original-parameter
hypotheses remain explicit. The earlier approved corrections and the
whole-field boundary of 3.17 are recorded in the
[paper corrections](https://github.com/daviddhilbert/integration-in-ACVF/blob/main/PAPER-CORRECTIONS.md)
and the section status files.
